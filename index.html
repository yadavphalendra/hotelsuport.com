Before you book

Good to know for a smooth stay

A few practical points that help avoid surprises at check-in.

Look beyond the nightly rate

Taxes and mandatory fees, such as resort, destination or amenity fees, can add significantly to the total. Compare the full price, not just the headline rate.

Know your cancellation deadline

Many rates allow free cancellation only until a set time before arrival, while prepaid or non-refundable rates may not allow changes at all.

Bring the right card and ID

Hotels usually ask for a government-issued photo ID and a payment card at check-in, and many place a temporary hold for incidentals.

Check the minimum check-in age

Many properties require the person checking in to be at least 18 or 21. Make sure the reservation is in the right name.

Make special requests early

Requests like adjoining rooms, cribs, high floors or early check-in are noted with the hotel but are subject to availability on arrival.

Confirm the exact location

Similar hotel names can be miles apart. Double-check the address and how you'll get there from the airport or venue.

Questions

Frequently asked

Am I speaking with the hotel when I call?

No. HotelSuport is an independent hotel booking assistance service. When you call 877-360-4334, you reach our own reservations agents — not a hotel's front desk or a hotel brand's call center. We are not affiliated with any hotel or hotel brand.

Why don't you list room rates on the website?

Hotel rates change daily — sometimes hourly — depending on dates, demand, room type and the rate's conditions. Rather than show prices that may already be out of date, our agents check what's actually available for your stay and share the full cost before anything is reserved.

Is there a charge for asking questions?

No. Calling or sending an enquiry is free and doesn't commit you to a booking. If you choose to reserve or change a stay through us, a service fee may apply; it is always explained and agreed with you before you pay.

Can you help with a reservation I already have?

In many cases, yes. Share your reservation details and we'll explain what changes or cancellations are possible. The options depend on the rate you booked and the property's own policies.

Do you arrange rooms for weddings, events or groups?

Yes. We can help you request multiple rooms for groups, family gatherings, weddings, sports teams or business events. Let us know your dates and number of rooms as early as you can.

What does the total price usually include?

Depending on the property and location, a hotel stay can include the room rate, taxes, and mandatory property fees such as resort or destination fees, plus optional costs like parking. We break down what's included before you decide.
