Before you call

Am I calling the hotel?

No. HotelSuport is an independent booking assistance service with its own agents.

Is there a fee?

A service fee may apply to reservations or changes. You'll be told the amount and asked to agree before paying.

What should I have ready?

Destination, check-in and check-out dates, number of rooms and guests, and the guest's name as shown on their ID.
