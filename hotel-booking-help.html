Our Services

Personal assistance with every part of your hotel stay, from the first search to the day you check out.

New hotel reservations

Tell us where you're going, when, and what matters most — location, budget, room type, amenities. Your agent checks currently available properties and explains the differences so you can choose with confidence.

  • City hotels, resorts, boutique and extended-stay properties
  • Domestic U.S. and international destinations
  • Single rooms, suites and multi-room bookings

Changing an existing reservation

Need to shift your dates, add a night or switch room type? We review the conditions attached to your rate and explain what's possible, including any difference in price and our service fee, before we make a change.

Cancellation and refund guidance

Hotel rates come with very different cancellation terms. We help you understand your deadline, any penalty and whether a refund applies, then guide you through the next steps. See our Cancellation Policy.

Group, wedding and event room blocks

Coordinating rooms for a wedding, family reunion, sports team, conference or tour group takes planning. We help you request multiple rooms, compare options near your venue and keep guest details organized.

Extended and long stays

Relocating, working on a project or caring for family? We can look for properties suited to longer visits, such as rooms with kitchenettes or laundry access, where available.

Business travel stays

Hotels near offices, convention centers or airports, with the amenities business travelers rely on, plus clear information on what your rate includes.

Accessibility and special requests

Accessible rooms, roll-in showers, connecting rooms, cribs or early check-in — we pass your requests to the property at booking. Final arrangements are made by the hotel under its own policies and availability.

Understanding rates, taxes and fees

We break down the total cost of a stay: room rate, taxes, mandatory property fees (such as resort or destination fees), deposit requirements and optional extras like parking, so there are no surprises at check-out.

Room rates, availability and property policies change often and are confirmed only at the time of reservation. HotelSuport is an independent service and is not a hotel or hotel brand.
