About HotelSuport

Hospitality starts before you arrive. We make planning your stay simple, clear and personal.

Our story

Searching for a hotel online can mean dozens of tabs, confusing rate rules and fees that only appear at the last step. HotelSuport offers a calmer alternative: a conversation with a reservations agent who takes the time to understand your trip and explain your options clearly.

We help leisure travelers, families, business guests and event organizers find suitable hotel stays, and we assist with changes, cancellations and questions about existing reservations.

What guides our work

Genuine attention

We listen first, then recommend options that fit your plans.

Clear costs

Rates, taxes, property fees and our service fee are explained before you pay.

Honest guidance

We explain cancellation terms and hotel policies in plain language.

Discretion

Your details are used only to arrange your stay, as our Privacy Policy describes.

Who we are — and who we are not

We are

  • An independent hotel booking assistance service
  • A team of reservations agents available by phone and email
  • Paid through service fees disclosed before you buy

We are not

  • A hotel, resort or the front desk of any property
  • Affiliated with, endorsed by or part of any hotel brand
  • Able to override a property's policies or rate conditions

You are always free to contact a hotel directly through its official website or published phone number.

How we're paid

We charge a service fee for researching, arranging or changing reservations, and in some cases we may receive commissions from accommodation suppliers. Any service fee you pay to us is shown separately from the room rate, taxes and property fees, and is agreed with you before you complete a booking.
